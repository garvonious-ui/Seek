How it works

1. SHARE

Say what's on your heart.

Joy, gratitude, struggle, seeking. Whatever the moment — type it in your own words.

2. RECEIVE

Scripture that meets you there.

Three to five verses chosen for the moment, a short prayer, and a worship song to carry with you.

3. SAVE

Keep what moves you.

Create a beautiful card to share, favorite the verses you return to, and build a library of what's touched you.
