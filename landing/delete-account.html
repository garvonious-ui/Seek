Delete your Seek account

You can permanently delete your Seek account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Fastest — in the app
  1. Open Seek and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Tap the profile icon in the top-right of the Home screen.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

By email

If you can't access the app, email hello@askseekpray.app from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account." We'll verify and delete your account within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile, preferences, streak, and usage history
  • Any saved cards, favorites, and prayers stored on your device (removed when you delete the app, or immediately on account deletion)

What we never stored

Seek does not keep the contents of your chat messages on its servers. Your conversations are processed to find scripture and are not retained server-side. We may keep anonymized, aggregate logs that cannot be tied to you, and limited records we are legally required to retain.

Questions? Reach us any time at hello@askseekpray.app.
